**Long description:**
Recordio is a cloud-based screen recorder built for teams that make product walkthroughs, demos, onboarding videos and async updates.

Record from the Chrome extension (also works on Edge and Brave, on macOS, Windows, Linux and ChromeOS). Recordio reads the page as you record, so it knows which input, card or menu you are using. Zooms frame the element rather than chasing the cursor, and every zoom is an editable keyframe. Auto spotlight lifts the element you interact with. Smart AutoCut removes silence but keeps typing and clicking pauses. A simplified toolbar hides your bookmarks, tabs and extensions from the recording.

In the web app you can blur sensitive regions, add a webcam bubble, put the recording in a MacBook frame, pick a background and music, and generate captions with word-level timing. Edit a caption line to fix it, or delete it to cut that part of the video.

Share a link and see view counts and watch time. Team workspaces come with roles, a shared library and unlimited viewer seats.

Recordio also captures screenshots: visible area, full page or a region. Annotate with text, arrows, shapes and blur, then copy, download as PNG or PDF, or share a link.

Pricing: Free plan with up to 5 active projects, 7-day retention and 1080p downloads. Pro is $12 per seat per month billed annually ($15 monthly) and adds 4K export, captions, share links with analytics, the team library, unlimited viewer seats, cloud rendering, unlimited projects and no expiry. An account is required; no card is needed for Free.

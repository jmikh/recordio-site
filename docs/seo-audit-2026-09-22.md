# Recordio.io SEO audit — 22 September 2026

Scope: recordio.io marketing site (Astro, 22 indexable pages, Cloudflare Pages), Google Analytics (Recordio Landing property), Google Search Console (sc-domain:recordio.io), live crawl of every page and every link, Lighthouse on the homepage and the Linux page, and a source review of every page and component.

Sections 1 to 3 are the findings. Section 4 lists what was changed in this pass (no approval needed). Section 5 lists what needs your decision or a dashboard action. Section 6 is the recommended plan.

---

## 1. Executive summary

**Health: technically sound, commercially invisible outside the brand name.** Every page is indexed, self-canonical, mobile-crawled, with one H1 and no heading skips. Lighthouse SEO and accessibility score 100. But the site earns essentially no non-brand search traffic, and the pages built to earn it (10 compare pages, 4 platform pages) are thin, orphaned, and until today carried retired pricing and positioning that contradicted the pricing card on the same page.

Nearly every click is branded ("recordio", "record io", "record.io", "recorderio"). Over 90 days the site earned **zero clicks on any non-brand query**. Traffic mix in GA over 90 days: 355 direct, 126 Google organic, 55 Google paid, 23 Bing organic, 10 Chrome Web Store referral, 8 ChatGPT.

### Top five issues

1. **Compare pages contradicted the product.** 8 of 10 still sold "$48/yr or $89 lifetime", all 10 claimed "local processing" and "captions in 99 languages", 5 led with "no per-seat pricing". The pricing block three sections lower said $12 per seat. Fixed in this pass (see section 4).
2. **No non-brand landing page has enough content to rank.** The Linux page (550 impressions in 90 days, position 21, 0.9% CTR) is 3% Linux-specific copy; the rest is the homepage's components. The compare hub has 14 words of body text and the platforms hub has 12.
3. **Internal linking is footer-only.** The header has no site links. Zero in-body links on the homepage, every compare page and every platform page. The platforms hub had no inbound links at all. Seven compare pages and both blog posts have exactly one inbound link.
4. **Mobile LCP is 6.6 s** (Lighthouse mobile perf 75). Cause: a 478 KB PNG hero and 18 image preload tags (React 19 SSR hoists a preload for every eager image, and Cloudflare turns them into Early Hints). Fixed in this pass: WebP hero with srcset, lazy-loaded testimonials. Expect LCP to drop well under 3 s after deploy.
5. **Site-wide schema was wrong.** SoftwareApplication offered Pro at $72/year; FAQPage was emitted on 17 pages that have no FAQ. Fixed in this pass.

### Quick wins already applied

WebP hero, lazy testimonial images, correct pricing schema, FAQ schema scoped, 404 noindexed, /sitemap.xml redirect, Chrome Web Store URL no longer 301s, privacy-page broken link, keyword-first titles and trimmed descriptions on all 22 pages, footer links to the platforms hub and three more compare pages, blog Article schema with image and logo, og:type=article on posts, compare-page claims corrected.

### 2.2 Performance and Core Web Vitals (Lighthouse, local run; PageSpeed Insights quota was exhausted)

| Page | Perf | LCP | FCP | TBT | CLS |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| / mobile | 75 | 6.6 s | 2.0 s | 50 ms | 0 |
| / desktop | 100 | 0.8 s | 0.5 s | 0 | 0 |
| /for/linux/ mobile | 77 | 4.9 s | 2.2 s | 20 ms | 0.08 |

Causes and status:

- **Hero image**: `hero-editor.png`, 2872 px, 478 KB. Now `hero-editor.webp` (43 KB) with a 1440 px variant (18 KB) via srcset. Largest single win.
- **18 image preload tags in the head**, echoed by Cloudflare as HTTP 103 Early Hints, for testimonial avatars and logos that sit far below the fold. React 19 SSR emits a preload for every non-lazy `<img>`. Adding `loading="lazy"` to the Testimonials images dropped the count to 7, all above the fold.
- Google Tag Manager script is 194 KB with 77 KB unused. Already deferred to idle; acceptable.
- Fonts: three self-hosted Satoshi woff2 files preloaded, `font-display: swap`. Fine.
- Feature videos: 25 MB in /public/videos but loaded only on intersection. Fine. Consider poster frames so the placeholder is not a blank box.
- HTML cache-control is `max-age=0, must-revalidate` and static assets are 4 hours. Consider `immutable` for `/_astro/*` hashed files via `public/_headers`.
- Best-practices 100, accessibility 100.

### 2.3 Structured data

- **SoftwareApplication** (every page): offered Pro at "$72 / P1Y", described the product as "a Chrome extension that turns raw recordings into professional videos", operatingSystem "Chrome, Edge, Brave". Now: Free $0, Pro $12/seat/month annual and $15 monthly via UnitPriceSpecification, description aligned with team/walkthrough positioning, OS lists browsers and platforms.
- **FAQPage** (was on every page): emitted on compare, blog, legal and 404 pages that render no FAQ, which violates the FAQ rich-result guideline. Now generated from a single `src/data/faq.ts` used by both the FAQ component and the schema, and emitted only on the 5 pages that render the FAQ.
- **Article** (blog posts): had no `image` and no `publisher.logo`, which Google requires for Article rich results. Added, plus the author's LinkedIn URL.
- Missing: an `Organization` block with `logo`, `sameAs` (Chrome Web Store, LinkedIn) and `contactPoint`; `BreadcrumbList` on compare, platform and blog pages. Both are cheap adds.

## 3. On-page and content findings

### 3.1 Titles and descriptions (all rewritten in this pass)

Before: homepage title 75 chars, 7 titles over 60 chars, 7 descriptions over 160. All 10 compare titles led with a slogan ("Screen Recordings That Sell Themselves — Loom Alternative") with no brand and the query at the end where it truncates. After: every title is keyword-first, brand-last, 60 chars or fewer; every description 155 or fewer. Full list in section 4.

### 3.2 Keyword opportunities visible in Search Console (90 days)

| Cluster | Impressions | Position | Landing page | Problem |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Linux/Ubuntu screen recorder (30+ query variants) | ~600 | 10 to 30 | /for/linux/ | Page is 97% shared homepage components; 0.9% CTR |
| "screen record io", "screen recorder io", "screen io" | ~90 | 9 to 45 | / | Near-brand; homepage title now includes "Screen Recorder" |
| Bandicam alternative / apps like Bandicam | ~35 | 70 to 80 | **/compare/** (not /compare/bandicam/) | Bandicam page had "Bandicam" only as a title suffix and no prose about Bandicam |
| Loom alternatives | few | 87 | blog | Blog post and /compare/loom/ do not link to each other |
| "best tutorial software" | 1 | 10 | / | Accidental; a walkthrough/tutorial angle would fit positioning |

### 3.3 Content depth

- **/for/linux/**: unique copy is a tag, an H1 and a 40-word subheadline. Add, between the hero and the feature videos: a "works on any distro" paragraph (Ubuntu, Fedora, Debian, Arch, Mint; needs Chrome, Chromium, Edge or Brave; no PPA/Snap/Flatpak); a Wayland vs X11 note (tab and window capture are the reliable path; verify full-screen behaviour with the product before publishing); a Recordio vs OBS vs SimpleScreenRecorder vs Kazam table linking to /compare/obs/; a four-step "record a walkthrough on Ubuntu" section; and a Linux-specific FAQ (Wayland, PipeWire/PulseAudio audio, no root needed) in place of the homepage FAQ.
- **/for/mac/, /for/windows/, /for/chrome/**: same pattern, same fix at lower priority. The Chrome page H1 says "for Chrome" while the query is "chrome extension".
- **/compare/bandicam/**: about 250 unique words. Add: who Bandicam is for and not for (Windows game/desktop capture, watermark on free, one-time licence) with "Bandicam alternative" in an H2; what Bandicam users gain (share link, team library, auto zoom, 1080p free / 4K Pro); what they lose (no game capture, no native-app hooks, needs an account); a pricing row; links to /compare/obs/, /compare/camtasia/, /for/windows/, /for/linux/.
- **Hubs**: /compare/ (14 words) and /for/ (12 words). Give each a real H1, an intro paragraph and a one-line blurb per linked page. Group compare links: async video (Loom, Tella, Clueso), polished recorders (Screen Studio, FocuSee), desktop capture (OBS, Camtasia, Bandicam), Chrome extensions (Screencastify, Screenity). Link both blog posts from the compare hub.
- **Competitor facts disagree between blog and compare pages**: the blog said Screen Studio is ~$9/mo annual and FocuSee is $199.99 one-time; compare pages said $209 one-time and ~$100 lifetime. Compare pages corrected to the blog's figures in this pass.

### 3.4 Internal linking

- Header nav is anchors plus "Open App" and "Add to Chrome" on every page. Compare, platform and blog pages are reachable only from the footer.
- Homepage body links to nothing. Compare pages link to no siblings, not to /compare/, not to the related blog post. Platform pages link to no siblings.
- Recommended: add Compare, Platforms and Blog to the header; an "Other comparisons" block before the CTA on every compare page (3 nearest siblings + the related post); a "Works on Mac, Windows, Linux, Chrome" strip under pricing on the homepage; a "Switching from Loom, Screen Studio or Bandicam?" strip under testimonials; render the Header on blog posts (currently only a "Back to Blog" link).

### 3.5 E-E-A-T

- Blog posts have a visible "Last updated" date, an author card with photo, title, bio and LinkedIn, and matching Article schema. Good.
- Gaps: no visible publish date (schema says 2026-03-05); author card is at the bottom; no About page; contact is a mailto only. Add `/about/` with founder bio and Organization/Person schema and link it from the footer and author cards.

### 3.6 Images

- 14 of 17 homepage images have empty alt. These are logos and avatars with adjacent visible or sr-only names, so empty alt is correct; not an issue.
- Only the hero and the author avatar had width/height. Testimonial avatars now have dimensions; logos vary in size and rely on the container height.
- No AVIF anywhere; WebP now used for the hero.

### 3.7 Dead code carrying retired copy

`src/components/TrustStrip.tsx` ("Local by Default", "Free 4K Export") and `src/components/Features.tsx` ("creators, educators") are not imported anywhere. Safe to delete; left in place for your call.

## 5. Needs your action or decision

### Dashboard actions (cannot be done from the repo)

1. **Search Console: request indexing** for `https://recordio.io/for/linux/`, `https://recordio.io/compare/screen-studio/` and `https://recordio.io/compare/camtasia/` (URL Inspection, "Request indexing"). This clears the stale recordio.cc canonicals; the Camtasia page is currently not indexed under recordio.io.
2. **Cloudflare: redirect www to apex.** Rules > Redirect Rules > Create: when `http.host eq "www.recordio.io"`, dynamic redirect to `concat("https://recordio.io", http.request.uri.path)`, status 301, preserve query string. The Pages `_redirects` file cannot match on host.
3. **Google Ads: pause or retarget the paid search campaign.** 55 sessions in 90 days, 14.5% engagement rate, **0 conversions**. Organic sessions to the same page convert at 26%.
4. **GA4: filter internal and dev traffic.** GA shows `localhost:3001` referrals (6 sessions) and a malformed landing page `/https://recordio.io`. Admin > Data Streams > Configure tag settings > Define internal traffic, then activate the filter.
5. **Cloudflare (optional): disable Email Address Obfuscation** under Scrape Shield so mailto links are plain links.
6. **Bing Webmaster Tools**: Bing sends 23 organic sessions per quarter and is not verified anywhere. Import the site from Search Console (one click).

### Decisions

7. **Header navigation**: add Compare, Platforms and Blog links. This is the single highest-leverage internal-linking change and it is a visible design change, so it is yours to approve.
8. **Content writing** for /for/linux/, /compare/bandicam/, and both hubs (section 3.3). The Wayland claim needs product verification.
9. **/about/ page** with founder bio and Organization schema.
10. **Delete dead components** `TrustStrip.tsx` and `Features.tsx`.
11. **Mobile visitors**: 145 mobile sessions in 90 days at 21% engagement and 7 conversions, because the CTA is a Chrome extension. Consider a mobile-specific CTA ("Email me the link") rather than sending them to the Web Store.
